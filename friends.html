official scrapbook rules

Friendship has rules.

  • Laugh at the same stupid joke at least ten times.
  • Take too many pictures because one day they will mean everything.
  • Turn ordinary days into stories worth remembering.
  • Never let a completely normal day stay completely normal.
  • Keep the random conversations going, even when there is nothing important to say.
  • Keep the people who feel like home close.
and most importantly... make more memories ♥
