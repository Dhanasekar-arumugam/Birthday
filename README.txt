## Setup

1. Put your photos in the `images` folder using the filenames above.
2. Open `script.js` and change:
   birthdayName: "Beautiful Soul"
   letterName: "Beautiful Soul"
3. Optional: put an MP3 in `music/birthday-song.mp3`, then uncomment/add the source line in `index.html`.
4. Open `index.html` in your browser.

## Notes

- The gallery is responsive for mobile, tablet and desktop.
- Photo cards open in a fullscreen lightbox.
- Keyboard arrows work in the lightbox.
- Gallery filters are included.
- The final surprise launches confetti and a heart animation.
